# Translation review, 2026-10-28

Review of the pt-BR, es and de interface texts of the editor (`src/i18n/locales`) and the agent
(`crates/vf-core/locales/*.ftl` in `vibefork-me/agent`) against English and Russian, with the context printed by
`npm run i18n:context -- --agent <agent repo>`: where each text is used, its role, the texts next to it,
length for narrow roles, the glossary terms and the form of address (`você`, `tú`, `du`).
Doubtful texts were translated back to English before deciding.

## Checked and kept

- **build — pt-BR/es "compilar, compilação/compilación"**: the established word for *build* in pt-BR and es
  software (also for projects that are not compiled); "construir" is literal and less recognised.
- **build — de "Build" (noun), "bauen" (verb)**: the usual pair in German software; noted in the glossary.
- **Journal — pt-BR/es "Registro"**: fits a panel of what happened; "Diário"/"Bitácora" sound like a diary or
  a ship's log. Kept, and the remaining "log" loanwords now say "Registro"/"Protokoll" (above).
- **de `card.forDevelopers` "Für Entwickler"**: the common UI wording; gender-neutral forms are a style
  decision for the whole German interface, not one text.
- **de `tray-quit` "Tray beenden", pt-BR/es "Fechar a bandeja"/"Cerrar la bandeja"**: they keep the point of
  the English — the tray icon closes, the agent keeps running (unlike "Stop the agent").
- **Length** (`--flags`): the other flagged buttons ("Noch einmal versuchen", "Intentar de nuevo",
  "Compilar de nuevo") sit in full-width cards or dialogs, not in fixed-width controls.
- **Placeholders, plurals**: every text uses the values of the English one (`check:i18n`); plural forms follow
  CLDR (pt/es `one`/`many`/`other`, de `one`/`other`).
- **Unused keys**: `common.appTitle`, `connectAi.moreAction`, `connectAi.option*Title/Description`,
  `connectAi.notInstalledNotice`, `connectAi.comingSoonNotice`, `open.workflowNotice`, `open.projectReady`,
  `open.build*`, `taskView.openArtifact`, `viewer.editorFailed` are not read by the code; reviewed only for
  errors.
